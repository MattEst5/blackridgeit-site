The broadcast layer

Stop rebuilding your stream every time the board changes.

A live card break already asks you to sell spots, track buyers, manage assignments, answer chat, and handle the cards. Manually editing graphics and juggling OBS scenes adds friction at the exact moment your show should feel fastest.

One show. Four clear phases.

Set up → Fill → Trade → Rip

Breaker Board Pro follows the way a break actually moves, so the operator view and the on-stream view stay in step.

  1. 01
    Set up

    Build or load the board.

    Choose the sport, load a customer Logo Pack, name the break, and prepare a Pick Your Team or Random Team workflow.

  2. 02
    Fill

    Show what is open.

    Mark teams or spots Available and Sold as the break fills, while the OBS board updates the audience-facing presentation.

  3. 03
    Trade

    Confirm who has what.

    Move to the Trade / Assignment Board to present buyer and team assignments before the cards are opened.

  4. 04
    Rip

    Put the cards back in focus.

Pre-launch FAQ

Questions before the board goes live.

Does Breaker Board Pro require a subscription?

No recurring subscription is planned for the core application. Breaker Board Pro is planned as a one-time purchase.

Do I need an account?

No. Breaker Board Pro is designed to run without a customer account.

Does it work offline?

Normal use is designed to operate offline after license activation.

Does it integrate directly with Whatnot?

No. Whatnot runs the sale, while Breaker Board Pro runs the local board and broadcast graphics. Breaker Board Pro is not affiliated with or endorsed by Whatnot.

Does it randomize teams?

No. Random Team results are generated with an external randomization service and then entered into Breaker Board Pro for assignment and presentation.

Does it include official team logos?

No. Official league and team logos are not bundled. You can import your own Logo Packs, and you are responsible for having rights to the artwork you use.

Do I need OBS?

OBS or another streaming application that supports Browser Sources is needed to place Breaker Board Pro graphics on a live broadcast.

Can I use my own logos?

Yes. Customer-created Logo Packs can use PNG, JPG, WebP, or SVG artwork. You are responsible for rights to imported artwork.

Is Mac supported?

Not yet. The first commercial launch target is Windows. A macOS version is planned, but native certification is still pending.

How will activation work?

The launch plan uses one-time license activation. An internet connection will be needed to activate, and normal use is designed to work offline afterward. Final details will be published at launch.
