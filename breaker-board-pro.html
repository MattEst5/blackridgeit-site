The broadcast layer

Stop rebuilding your stream every time the board changes.

A live card break already asks you to sell spots, track buyers, manage assignments, answer chat, and handle the cards. Manually editing graphics and juggling OBS scenes adds friction at the exact moment your show should feel fastest.

Breaker Board Pro keeps show control in one place. The local Break Desk runs the sports card break board, and polished Browser Source graphics carry team availability and buyer/team assignments into OBS.

One show. Four clear phases.

Set up → Fill → Trade → Rip

Breaker Board Pro follows the way a break actually moves, so the operator view and the on-stream view stay in step.

  1. 01
    Set up

    Build or load the board.

    Choose a sport, add optional customer artwork, and set the title, product/year, and break number. Prepare a Pick Your Team or externally assigned Random Team board.

  2. 02
    Fill

    Show what is open.

    Use the Fill Board as a team availability board, then mark teams or spots Sold as the break fills while the audience-facing OBS board updates.

  3. 03
    Trade

    Confirm who has what.

    Move to the Trade / Assignment Board to present buyer and team assignments before the cards are opened.

  4. 04
    Rip

    Put the cards back in focus.

    Switch to Camera or Hidden without rebuilding your OBS scene. Keep statuses, alerts, ticker messages, or the optional standalone camera frame available as needed.

Random Team results are generated externally, then entered into Breaker Board Pro for assignment and presentation.

Product FAQ

Know what you’re bringing to the break.

Does Breaker Board Pro require a subscription?

No subscription is required for the core standalone application. The launch price is $59.99 one-time; the regular price is $79.99. Windows checkout is coming soon. A future optional marketplace/API integration could have a separate subscription if appropriate third-party access becomes available; no integration or subscription price is currently available.

Do I need an account?

No account is required for normal operation. Breaker Board Pro keeps application state locally, with no telemetry and no cloud heartbeat.

Does normal operation require Internet?

Normal use works offline after activation, with no required recurring Internet connection. Your streaming platform still needs an Internet connection to broadcast.

Does it work with Whatnot?

Breaker Board Pro works independently alongside live-selling workflows such as Whatnot, using local boards and OBS presentation. The marketplace handles transactions; Breaker Board Pro handles broadcast control. Direct Whatnot API integration, automatic sales synchronization, and native account connectivity are not currently available. BlackRidge IT is not affiliated with or endorsed by Whatnot.

Does it randomize teams?

No. Generate Random Team results externally, then enter or import the assignments into Breaker Board Pro for presentation. Breaker Board Pro does not process payments, marketplace orders, bids, or auctions.

Does it include official team logos?

No. Official league and team logos are not bundled. Customers may import artwork they have rights to use. The Logo Pack demonstration shows customer-supplied artwork, not included assets or licensed league content from BlackRidge IT.

Do I need OBS?

To place Breaker Board Pro graphics on a live broadcast, use OBS or another streaming application that supports Browser Sources. Breaker Board Pro supplies local Browser Source URLs; you control the presentation in the Break Desk. The main source supports Fill, Trade, Camera, and Hidden without rebuilding the scene. An optional standalone camera-frame source is also available.

Can I use my own artwork?

Yes. Import PNG, JPG/JPEG, WebP, or SVG team and spot artwork. Breaker Board Pro optimizes imported images locally and saves Logo Packs for reuse across future breaks. Customers are responsible for having rights to artwork they import.

Is macOS supported?

Not commercially at this time. Windows is the current launch platform. macOS is planned, but is not currently commercially available or certified.

How does activation work?

Activation uses a manual one-time activation request and a BlackRidge-issued license file. Once activated, normal operation works offline without an account or recurring Internet connection.

What does the one-time license cover?

The purchased major version is a perpetual license for one active installation/device. Maintenance and bugfix updates for v1 are included, with reasonable legitimate license reissues available. Future major-version pricing has not been committed.

How will I purchase and receive the Windows app?

Windows purchase availability is pending final commerce approval. Once checkout is approved, this page will provide the purchase link and information on obtaining the Windows application and completing activation. For now, contact BlackRidge IT with launch or setup questions.
